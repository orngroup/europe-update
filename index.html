Structure

How the operation is structured

The European office sits between the group's source markets and the European supplier base, owning the relationship on both sides.

Group benefit: four markets served from one cost base; the group owns European product rather than buying it at arm's length; faster payments at lower cost, with full transparency in Xero and monthly reporting to ORN Nexus PTE and Bangalore.

ORN Cruise

Building ORN Cruise

A dedicated cruise business, built on real cruise-industry expertise and direct line relationships.

ORN Cruise is being established as a solid, standalone cruise operation within the group — contracting, cruise-line relationships, charter negotiation and allocation, with a particular focus on focused destination cruises and Evergreen product. The ambition is to move from sourcing cruise as required to owning a genuine cruise business with real margin and direct supplier relationships.

The build is led by senior cruise-industry expertise — a major cruise advisor and head of business from one of the largest cruise companies now working with ORN to grow and establish ORN Cruise, alongside Manish's three decades in travel and cruising.

Curated, destination-led cruise programmes built as owned product.

Evergreen product

Year-round, repeatable cruise programmes distributed across every ORN office.

Quality & customer support

Built-in quality control

Quality is built into the process, not checked at the end.

The operation runs on a fully built operations platform where every European booking is tracked, reconfirmed and confirmed in one place. Every component of every booking is reconfirmed directly with the supplier against a structured 6-point check — operator, contact, confirmation, time and pick-up location — before the guest travels, so nothing is left to chance.

24-hour customer support

Customers get support exactly as they do today with the Australia/Oceania operation — the identical support telephone system, with a dedicated European number.
